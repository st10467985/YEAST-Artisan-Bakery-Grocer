Company History

YEAST Artisan Bakery & Grocer is an independent bakery and grocery business serving customers in Bedfordview and Dunvegan, Edenvale.

YEAST focuses on artisan baking and fresh grocery products, with products prepared using quality ingredients.

Mission

YEAST aims to be known for quality artisan baking within the local community and for providing quality produce through its independent grocery offering.

Artisan Baking

YEAST's bakers prepare products from scratch each day rather than using pre-mixes. The bakery uses a unique dough starter as part of its artisan baking approach.

The bakery offers a variety of products including sourdough bread, ciabatta, rye bread, French sourdough baguettes, gluten-free bread, vegan-friendly bread, cakes, Chelsea buns, doughnuts, cupcakes and quiches.

Why Choose YEAST?

YEAST provides customers with artisan bakery products and grocery products while serving the local community. Its range of products and two Gauteng locations make it a convenient option for customers looking for fresh bakery and grocery items.
